# Terms of Service Change Tracker

The **ToS Change Tracker** monitors specified Terms of Service (ToS) or Privacy Policy pages for updates and alerts on detected text changes or keyword mentions.

## Features:
1. **Detect Text Changes:** Monitors pages for any modifications to content.
2. **Keyword Matching:** Flags important keywords related to data privacy, sharing, and legal clauses.
3. **Alerts on Changes and Keywords:** Tracks and highlights changes with excerpts of old and new content.

## Outputs:
- `url`: The monitored URL.
- `changeDetected`: Boolean flag for content updates.
- `keywordsMatched`: List of flagged keywords found in changes.
- `alertFlag`: Boolean to indicate if an alert was generated.

## Ethical Use:
- Use with explicit permission or open/publicly available pages.
- Comply with local regulations and privacy policies.
